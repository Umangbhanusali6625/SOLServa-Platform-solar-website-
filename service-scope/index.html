Service Scope & Limitations

Operational Parameters • Technical Guidelines • Last updated: October 2026

Please note the following service parameters and operational guidelines for SOLServa monitoring systems:

1. Data Telemetry & Update Frequencies

Telemetry updates operate in near real-time (5-15 min updates) depending on the contracted service tier, local cellular 4G/WiFi coverage, and inverter polling frequency. Actual monitored electrical metrics (voltage, current, power, frequency, alarms) depend on inverter protocol capabilities and firmware configuration.

2. Inverter Compatibility

Inverter models and OEM cloud platforms are evaluated on an individual site-by-site basis. While our engineering team supports major industry protocols (including Sungrow, Growatt, Solis, Polycab, Sofar, GoodWe, Huawei, and others), universal compatibility across all legacy firmware revisions is not implied. A technical compatibility audit is performed prior to hardware dispatch.

3. Hardware Logging & Warranty Guidelines

SOLServa hardware loggers communicate via non-intrusive standard RS-485 serial interfaces or authorized USB ports. Plant asset owners should verify manufacturer terms to ensure third-party passive data acquisition conforms to OEM warranty documentation.

4. O&M Service Delivery & Field Coordination

Physical maintenance actions, thermal imaging audits, string testing, inverter repairs, and module washing are governed by contracted service tiers, geographic availability, and executed service agreements. Digital monitoring alerts asset owners promptly to enable timely resolution.

5. Operational Boundaries & Generation Disclaimer

Telemetry monitoring systems record and report plant electrical behavior; they do not generate power. Actual solar harvest is influenced by environmental irradiance, temperature, soiling, shading, and equipment health. SOLServa provides transparent diagnostic visibility to minimize downtime.
