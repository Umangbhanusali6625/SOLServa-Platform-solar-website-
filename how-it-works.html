How SOLServa Monitoring Works

From first enquiry to active monitoring and ongoing service tracking, our standardized 6-step lifecycle ensures full transparency.

1

Enquiry

Customer or EPC submits plant location, capacity, and inverter specifications.

2

Review

We review inverter communication protocols, existing loggers, and data access.

3

Proposal

Confirmation of data scope, hardware requirements (if needed), and transparent pricing.

4

Onboarding

Authorized access configuration, logger connectivity, and alert recipient setup.

5

Monitoring

Telemetry collection, daily summaries, and performance ratio tracking at agreed frequency.

6

Service Action

Abnormal alerts trigger service ticket creation and maintenance coordination.

*Note: The exact technical process may vary depending on inverter brand, logger hardware, local cellular network, and contracted service plan.

Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

SOLServa collects contact details (name, email, phone, business affiliation) submitted via our enquiry, compatibility, and partner application forms. When providing telemetry monitoring, technical inverter operational metrics (voltages, current, kWh generation, fault codes) are processed.

2. Security & Passwords

We never request or store inverter user passwords in open web forms. Integration is achieved via authorized OEM API keys or local hardware interface access.

3. Data Usage

Customer contact information and plant performance data are utilized solely for service delivery, reporting, and technical diagnostics. We do not sell or rent data to third parties.
