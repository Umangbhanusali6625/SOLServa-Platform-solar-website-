Solar plant monitoring is the continuous collection, processing, and visualization of electrical data from inverters, weather stations, and energy meters. It enables plant owners and EPCs to observe daily generation, verify performance against expectations, and detect faults or communication losses promptly.

Monitoring is the passive detection and recording of operational data and alarms. O&M (Operation & Maintenance) encompasses physical actions, including preventative maintenance, thermography, string testing, inverter repairs, and module cleaning. SOLServa provides monitoring and coordinates O&M ticketing according to your service contract.
Monitoring itself does not produce electricity; generation depends on solar irradiance, equipment specifications, site shading, and cleanliness. What SOLServa guarantees is prompt visibility of faults, reduced downtime through swift alerting, and transparent performance data to guide maintenance.
